01 / Architecture

A pipeline built for traceability, not just a one-time result.

Each layer has one job. Raw evidence remains immutable, transformations stay inspectable, and the final table is shaped for analysis.

01data/bronze

Preserve the source

Daily order captures are stored exactly as received, checksummed with SHA-256, and logged without silent overwrites.

  • Atomic, idempotent writes
  • Append-only audit history
  • Schema and content checks
→
02data/silver

Reconcile the detail

Changing product systems are mapped to canonical IDs through weighted scoring followed by an auditable human decision for every uncertain candidate.

  • 60 automatic matches
  • 20 flagged matches reviewed
  • 6 suggestions overridden
→
03data/gold

Publish with confidence

Sales are aggregated by date, store, and product, then joined to store dimensions and daily weather.

  • Analytics-ready grain
  • CSV and MariaDB outputs
  • Pre-publish validation gate

02 / Engineering quality

The pipeline fails loudly when the data is wrong.

Validation is part of the product. Empty downloads, HTML responses, unexpected columns, invalid dates, conflicting captures, incomplete joins, and broken totals stop publication.

The same discipline applies to entity resolution: every flagged product match has a recorded decision and rationale, and the pipeline consumes only the approved canonical mapping.
